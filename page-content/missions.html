What Is Faith Promise?

Faith Promise is a method of giving where each church member prayerfully asks God what amount He would have them give above their tithe specifically for missions — trusting God to provide that amount throughout the year.

It is not a pledge of personal funds, but a promise of faith that God will provide the resources. This model has sent the gospel to unreached peoples across the globe for generations.

We believe in the Great Commission — that God has given the Church the command to proclaim the gospel to all nations, tribes, ethnic groups, and language groups.
