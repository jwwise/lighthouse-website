Our Pastor

Pastor Steve & Pasqua Hensley

Pastor Steve Hensley

Pastor Steve Hensley was saved on May 9, 1992, and felt called to preach shortly after his salvation. He spent 23 years pastoring in Tennessee before answering God's call to Lighthouse Independent Baptist Church in Payette, Idaho in 2018.

Pastor Hensley is an expository preacher — committed to taking the congregation through books and passages of the Bible verse by verse, trusting that "all Scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness" (2 Timothy 3:16).

Pasqua Hensley

Pasqua Hensley was saved in 1980 and has been a faithful helpmeet to Pastor Hensley throughout his ministry. Together they have three daughters and seven grandchildren, and they are grateful for the family God has given them through the congregation at Lighthouse.

"Feed the flock of God which is among you, taking the oversight thereof, not by constraint, but willingly; not for filthy lucre, but of a ready mind."

— 1 Peter 5:2
