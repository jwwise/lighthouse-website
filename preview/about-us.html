About Us

A community grounded in Scripture, built on faith

Who We Are

Lighthouse Independent Baptist Church is a King James Bible-believing, independent Baptist church in Payette, Idaho. We are a traditional, family-oriented congregation committed to expository preaching, evangelism, and discipleship.

We believe the local church is the pillar and ground of the truth, and we are dedicated to reaching our community and the world with the gospel of Jesus Christ through preaching, teaching, and evangelism.

Whether you are a lifelong believer or just beginning to explore faith, you are welcome here. We invite you to join us for any of our services.
