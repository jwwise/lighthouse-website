Church History

Faithful from the beginning

December 28, 2024

Interim Pastor: Mike Rue

The congregation voted to ask Missionary Mike Rue to serve as Interim Pastor while the pulpit committee seeks God's will for permanent leadership. Bro. Rue — a church planter with Calvary Baptist Missions who had been working alongside Pastor Hensley — has been faithfully preaching and shepherding the congregation through this season of transition.

December 14, 2024

In Memoriam: Pastor Steve Hensley

Pastor Steve Hensley passed away on December 14, 2024, after faithfully serving Lighthouse Baptist Church since 2018. His memorial service was held on December 18, 2024 at the church in Payette, Idaho. A funeral service followed on December 26, 2024 at Mt. Pisgah Baptist Church in Olive Springs, Tennessee, with a graveside service the next day at Western Cemetery in Coalfield, Tennessee. Watch the memorial service.

November 22, 2017

Fifth Pastor: Steve Hensley

The church voted to accept Pastor Steve Hensley as their fifth pastor. In September of 2018, Pastor Hensley and his wife Pasqua moved from Tennessee to take the pastorate of Lighthouse Baptist Church — where they faithfully serve today.

February 4, 2017

In Memoriam: Pastor McComb

Pastor Bruce McComb passed away, leaving behind a legacy of faithful service to the congregation he loved.

February 23, 2011

Fourth Pastor: Bruce McComb

The church voted to accept Pastor Bruce McComb as their fourth pastor. His wife's name is Nancy.

July 23, 2008

Returning to Payette

The property in Ontario was sold and the Payette property was purchased — bringing the church back to its roots. In August of 2010, Pastor Elrod resigned to move back to Michigan.

December 31, 2006

Third Pastor: Tim Elrod

Pastor Boss resigned to move back to Sutherland, Oregon. Pastor Tim Elrod was voted in as the third pastor of Lighthouse Baptist Church. His wife's name is Keren.

May 4, 2003

Second Pastor: Donald Boss

Pastor Keaton resigned and Pastor Donald Boss was voted in as the church's second pastor. His wife's name was Rosa.

Early 2003

Ontario, Oregon

A 3-bedroom house on 1.3 acres in Ontario, Oregon was purchased and services began. The first business meeting was held in late February 2003. The church voted to organize, Pastor Keaton was voted in as first pastor, and in April, 19 members signed the charter.

Early 2001

Moving to Fruitland

A small store-front building was rented in Fruitland, Idaho, and services were held there for two years as the congregation continued to grow.

May 1999

A Church Is Planted

Missionary Clinton Keaton and his wife Dorothy planted Lighthouse Baptist Church in their home in Payette, Idaho. He was a missionary out of Bible Baptist Church in Mountain Home, Idaho. Services were held for nearly two years in their living room.
